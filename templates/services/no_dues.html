Library Clearance (No-Dues) Certificate Procedure

Official step-by-step instructions for diploma students completing their course, transferring, or receiving diplomas.

Important Mandate: A valid Library No-Dues Certificate is mandatory prior to collecting your BTEUP Final Diploma, Marksheets, or Migration Certificate from the Registrar's Office.
Step-by-Step Procedure
Step 1: Check Your Borrowing Records

Verify that all physical textbooks issued in your name have been returned to the circulation counter in good physical condition.

Step 2: Clear Pending Dues / Fines

Clear any outstanding overdue fines at the library accounts counter and retain your fee receipt.

Step 3: Return Library Membership Card

Surrender your laminated Central Library Membership Card and Book Bank cards to the circulation assistant.

Step 4: Receive Signed Clearance Certificate

Upon successful verification of records, the Librarian will stamp and sign your institutional clearance slip.

Lost or Damaged Book Policy

In the event of an accidental loss or damage to a library book, the student must either replace the volume with its latest edition or deposit the cost as assessed under institutional rules before clearance is granted.
