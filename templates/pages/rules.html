Institutional Code

Library Rules & Regulations

Guidelines to maintain a peaceful academic atmosphere, ensure equitable access, and protect institutional collection assets.

General Conduct & Discipline
  • College Identity Card Mandatory: Entry to the library is allowed strictly upon presenting a valid, non-expired College Identity Card at the entrance counter.
  • Silence Discipline: Strict silence must be maintained in the reading hall and stack areas at all times. Group discussions, loud whispering, and loitering are strictly prohibited.
  • Mobile Phone Policy: Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode inside the library premises. Attending calls inside the reading hall is not permitted.
  • Property Counter: Personal textbooks, personal bags, umbrellas, and printed notes must be deposited at the property counter before entering.
  • Food & Beverage Prohibition: Food items, tea, coffee, and soft drinks are strictly prohibited inside the library premises.

Care of Books, Loss & Damage Terms
  • Care of Materials: Borrowers are strictly responsible for maintaining books in good physical condition. Underlining, highlighting, folding pages, scribbling, or tearing pages is an offense.
  • Lost or Damaged Volumes: In case of loss or severe damage to a book, the borrower must replace the book with its latest edition or deposit the cost as assessed under institutional rules.
  • Clearance (No-Dues): A formal No-Dues Clearance Certificate will be issued only after all borrowed materials are returned and all dues/fines are cleared.
For special reference permissions, consult the Librarian.
