Strategic Direction

Vision, Mission & Core Objectives

Guiding principles shaping collection development and student support services at Government Polytechnic Madhogarh Central Library.

Our Vision

"To evolve as a benchmark polytechnic knowledge and resource center in Uttar Pradesh, empowering technical students with cutting-edge academic print collections, open digital resources, and inclusive technological learning environments."

Our Mission

  • To procure, organize, and disseminate standard curriculum textbooks and technical references aligned with BTEUP diploma syllabi.
  • To bridge the digital learning divide by providing free access to the National Digital Library of India, AICTE e-KUMBH portal, and NPTEL online courseware.
  • To foster an academic culture of peaceful reading, critical thinking, and disciplined intellectual curiosity.
  • To deliver user-friendly automated library services with transparency and equity for students and staff.
Core Library Objectives
Curricula Alignment

Ensure recommended textbooks per diploma subject across Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics Engineering branches.

Book Bank Inclusivity

Provide full semester textbook sets to economically vulnerable and meritorious diploma scholars.

Digital Literacy

Train 100% of enrolled diploma scholars to navigate online journals, NDLI e-resources, and automated OPAC.

Examination Support

Maintain an updated archive of previous 10 years of BTEUP question papers and model solution banks.
