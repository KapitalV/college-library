About Government Polytechnic Madhogarh Central Library

The academic and intellectual nerve center of Government Polytechnic Madhogarh, Kailor, District Jalaun (Uttar Pradesh).

Welcome to the Central Library of Government Polytechnic Madhogarh, Jalaun. The library serves as the academic and information hub of the institution, catering to the knowledge, learning, curricula, and research needs of diploma scholars, faculty, and technical staff.

The library houses an extensive collection of technical textbooks, reference handbooks, previous year examination question papers, and national educational periodicals mapped strictly to the Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP) diploma curriculum for Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics Engineering.

Key Features & Infrastructure
  • Spacious Reading Hall: A well-ventilated and quiet reading area with comfortable, ergonomic seating arrangements designed for uninterrupted self-study.
  • Library Automation & ERP: Integrated book catalogue (OPAC), campus ERP integration (erp.bizby.io), and NIC e-Granthalaya automation software.
  • Curricula-Mapped Collection: Dedicated textbook racks covering Computer Science & Engineering (Branch 355/389), Electrical Engineering (Branch 328/378), and Electronics Engineering (Branch 330/380).
  • Digital Integration: Direct access to digital e-books, the AICTE e-KUMBH portal (regional language technical books), National Digital Library of India (NDLI), and SWAYAM / NPTEL e-learning resources.
