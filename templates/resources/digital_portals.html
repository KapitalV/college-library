National Educational & Research Portals

Comprehensive guide to government-sponsored e-learning platforms accessible to polytechnic students and faculty.

National Digital Library of India (NDLI)
Ministry of Education & IIT Kharagpur

NDLI is a virtual repository of learning resources containing over 9.5 crore educational items including textbooks, simulation models, conference papers, video lectures, and rare manuscripts.

Access Classification: Open Access + Institutional Club Member
Format: Full-text PDF, Video Lectures, Interactive Datasets
Club Registration: Supported by GP Madhogarh Institutional Club Passkey
Visit NDLI Portal View Registration Guide
AICTE e-KUMBH Portal
All India Council for Technical Education

e-KUMBH (Knowledge Unleashed in Multiple Bharatiya Languages) delivers high quality engineering & diploma course textbooks translated into Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and other Indian languages.

Access Classification: Free Public Access (AICTE Initiative)
Target: Polytechnic Diploma & B.Tech Students
Languages: Hindi, English, and 10 Indian Regional Languages
Access e-KUMBH Portal
e-Granthalaya 4.0 (EG4)
National Informatics Centre (NIC)

e-Granthalaya 4.0 is a cloud-based library management platform developed by NIC to network government institutional libraries into a national union catalogue.

Access Classification: Public OPAC + Staff Intranet
Features: Union catalogue search, standard AACR2 / MARC-21 cataloging
Hosting: National Informatics Centre Cloud, New Delhi
Visit e-Granthalaya
SWAYAM & NPTEL MOOCs
Ministry of Education & IITs

SWAYAM hosts online courses from school level to post-graduate level, created by top educators from IITs, IIMs, and central universities with credit-transfer provisions.

Access Classification: Free Course Audit & Study Materials
Certification: Proctored exam with government-recognized certificate
Subjects: Engineering, Mathematics, Computer Programming, Humanities
Visit SWAYAM Portal
