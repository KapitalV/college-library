A Student’s Guide to Government Polytechnic Madhogarh Library

A Student’s Guide to GP Madhogarh Library

Government Polytechnic Madhogarh

Kailor, Madhogarh, District Jalaun, Uttar Pradesh – 285126

Welcome to the Government Polytechnic Madhogarh Library! The Central Library supports diploma scholars, engineering students, and faculty in their academic learning, technical curriculum, and information needs. This guide explains how students can explore Library resources and use the available services.

1. Explore Library Resources

Students are encouraged to use available resources for classroom learning, assignments, diploma laboratory work, projects, and self-study:

  • Textbooks and curriculum books for all 3 engineering departments: Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics Engineering.
  • Reference materials, technical manuals, and engineering handbooks.
  • BTEUP semester examination question papers and curriculum syllabus.
  • Digital learning repositories, e-books, and video lectures.
  • New arrivals and Library updates.

2. Search Books through OPAC

The Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC) helps students and faculty search the Library catalogue by book title, author name, subject, or accession number.

3. Digital Library & National Portals

Government Polytechnic Madhogarh provides integrated access to national digital learning repositories:

  • e-Granthalaya 4.0 (EG4) NIC Cloud: Institutional library automation and cloud catalogue (http://eg4.nic.in/).
  • National Digital Library of India (NDLI): Over 100+ million educational resources and e-books developed by IIT Kharagpur (ndl.iitkgp.ac.in).
  • SWAYAM & NPTEL: Free online video courses, lectures, and certifications by premier institutes (swayam.gov.in).
  • AICTE e-KUMBH Portal: Technical education books in Hindi and Indian regional languages (ekumbh.aicte-india.org).

4. BTEUP Question Papers & Syllabus

Semester examination papers and official BTEUP curriculum schemes are organized by branch:

5. Library Timings, Circulation & Borrowing Limits

Members must present their valid Institutional Identity Card / Library Card during circulation hours:

Category Loan Quota Loan Duration Renewal Policy
Regular Diploma Students 3 Books 14 Days 1 Renewal (if no reservation)
Book Bank Beneficiaries (SC/ST/Merit) Full Semester Set Full Semester Return post semester exams
Faculty & Instructional Staff 6 Books 30 Days Renewable

Welcome Newly Admitted Students – Academic Session 2026-27

The Central Library extends a warm welcome to all newly admitted diploma engineering students of Government Polytechnic Madhogarh, Kailor (Jalaun) for the academic session 2026-27.

All 1st year students enrolled in Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics Engineering are advised to complete their Library Card registration and NDLI Club enrollment at the earliest.

📌 Steps for Library Card Issuance:

  1. Collect the Library Registration Form from the circulation desk or download online via Library Services.
  2. Attach one passport-size photograph and copy of your JEECUP admission allotment letter / college fee receipt.
  3. Submit to the Librarian, Mrs. Priyanka Singh Dangi, at the Central Library counter.
  4. Activate your National Digital Library of India (NDLI) Club membership using Passkey: d3a479f5-9822-4888-a86e-eb91d907c873.
